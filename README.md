# Good Room flyers by Bráulio Amado

An unofficial homage to the flyers [Bráulio Amado](https://badbadbadbad.com/) designed for [Good Room](https://ra.co/clubs/97606), the Brooklyn club, from 2015 to 2026.

**[Open the flyer wall](https://orn3tte.github.io/good-room-flyers/)**

This is a fan project, not affiliated with Bráulio Amado or Good Room. The artwork is entirely his. This repository holds no images: the page shows each flyer directly from Resident Advisor, where it was first published, and links to its event.

## The page

The page shows 592 flyers by year, and lets you sort them into your own categories and filter by them. It works on a phone or a computer.

- Click flyers to select them, then add them to a category from the bar at the bottom.
- Click one or more categories to show only their flyers ("In any" or "In all").
- The magnifier opens a flyer at full size. On a computer, the arrow keys move between flyers and the number keys 1 to 9 put the flyer in or out of a category.
- Categories stay in the browser of the device you use. To move them to another device, use **Save backup** there and **Load backup** here.

| File | What it holds |

*Recto* is the front of a flyer, *verso* its back.
